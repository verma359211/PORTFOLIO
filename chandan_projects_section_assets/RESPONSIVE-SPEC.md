# Projects Section Responsive Specification

## Desktop — 1280px and above

- The introduction occupies the full width.
- Intro copy stays on the left and the CSS geometric decoration sits on the right.
- Use a two-column project grid.
- Banana Type and E-commerce form row one.
- DeployHub and Bug Tracker form row two.
- RAG Pipeline occupies the left column of row three.
- The closing CTA occupies the right column of row three.
- Each project card is split horizontally:
  - preview: approximately 53%
  - details: approximately 47%
- Maintain thick black borders and 8–10px hard offset shadows.
- Section content max-width: approximately 1500–1580px.

## Tablet — 700px to 1279px

- Use one project card per row.
- Preserve the internal side-by-side split where the card is at least 720px wide.
- At narrower tablet sizes, allow the preview to use 46–50% and details 50–54%.
- RAG and closing CTA each become full-width cards.
- Reduce offset shadows to 6–8px.
- Reduce intro decoration size and keep it out of the heading's reading area.

## Mobile — below 700px

- Use a single-column layout.
- Stack each preview above its details panel.
- Keep the project-number label attached to the top-left corner.
- Use 18–20px section padding.
- Use 18–22px card padding.
- Make GitHub and live buttons full-width or two equal columns.
- Technology items wrap into a 2-column grid or flexible rows.
- Use 5–6px hard shadows.
- Preserve visible borders and bold typography.
- No horizontal scrolling at 320px.
- Decorative top-right geometry may be simplified or hidden.
- The CTA card stacks its text and action.

## Required test sizes

- 1920 × 1080
- 1440 × 900
- 1366 × 768
- 1024 × 768
- 768 × 1024
- 390 × 844
- 320 × 700
